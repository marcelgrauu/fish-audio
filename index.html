The small print, because it matters

  • It really is free. The S2.1 Pro Free model is listed at $0.00 per million UTF-8 bytes, while every other model on the same page is $15.00.
  • The API is not the website quota. The 8,000 monthly credits you see on the plan page are for generating from their web app. The API is metered separately, with no subscription and no monthly minimum.
  • It has an end date. Fish Audio says free access runs through 30 November 2026 and that they will give notice before changing it.
  • Your requests may train their model. That is stated in their free tier policy. Worth knowing before you send your own voice.
